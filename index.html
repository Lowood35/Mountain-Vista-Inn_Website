Welcome to Mountain Vista Inn

Brought to you by the team behind Lowood Inn, a trusted name in hospitality since 2004, Mountain Vista Inn offers a peaceful stay just minutes from the iconic Dal Lake. Thoughtfully designed for comfort and convenience, the property features spacious rooms with private kitchen facilities, ideal for families and long stays. With the same dedication to warmth and genuine care that defines Lowood Inn, Mountain Vista Inn promises a cozy retreat amidst the charm of Srinagar, Kashmir.

Located within a short drive of Srinagar’s iconic attractions — the Mughal Gardens, Tulip Garden, Botanical Garden, Pari Mahal, and Nehru Park — Mountain Vista Inn offers the perfect base for exploring the beauty of the city.
